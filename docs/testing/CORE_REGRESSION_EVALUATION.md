## Coverage gaps and risks

- The candidate intentionally omits most frozen M7E–M7R evaluator/provenance Unit tests; the automatic B2 workflow is not equivalent to those Unit contracts.
- Unit tests do not prove public-browser transport, real rendered pixels, production-preview isolation, or native GPU execution.
- The `src/scenes/**` affected owner deliberately returns `INCOMPLETE` while selecting its known publication tests; scene geometry, material, task, and renderer dependencies are not fully mapped. This avoids a narrow PASS and avoids running unrelated WebGPU research suites by filename coincidence.
- The independent reviewer identified the missing direct `deriveOpticsState` test and the overconfident scene mapping. The candidate manifest now includes that optics test, and scene implementation changes report incomplete coverage. The earlier candidate report `2026-11-24T00-31-24-291Z-45687-1e2095e7` selected 47 files and predates the owner correction; it is superseded by the matched 01:11 report above, which selects 48 and includes `src/tests/unit/deriveOpticsState.test.ts`.
- The mandatory Chromium smoke checks public canvas/contentful diagnostics and Raw/Upright radio state. It is not independent pixel evidence for physical orientation or image-circle rendering. The candidate and future reduction must retain appropriate rendered-pixel, browser, and native-GPU proof for those claims.
