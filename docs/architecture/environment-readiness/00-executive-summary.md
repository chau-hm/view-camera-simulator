# Environment architecture and Ground Glass readiness

A0 audits the three outdoor environments and Table Tilt studio at `35212892d5a359213f68f984bc68ad47c4520886`. The delivery is documentation only. Zero Dream Loop cycles were run.

## Decisions

1. **Qualify diagnostics before opening physical render extent.** The high-tilt whole-image variance ratio is content-sensitive. The two historical courtyard previews fail at 0.46437 and 0.43892 with the current frame, full pass sequence and unchanged sampled teaching ROIs. Five present-day seeds at the regular clip passed, with ratios between 0.50195 and 0.50962. A1 should preserve the existing comparison during qualification and add current-request, actual execution, physical CoC, local response, depth rejection and orientation witnesses. [Ground Glass audit](02-ground-glass-regression-audit.md)
2. **Use the existing profile seam for opt-in hybrid extent.** Keep teaching bounds as the fallback. In the current courtyard, a local 13.53 to 17.4 m far-plane trial restored 395 of 5,120 calibrated film samples and left the 2,580 original-surface ray hits and film mapping unchanged. The same trial shifted paired GPU depth and sampled CoC and reduced the high-tilt ratio from 0.50962 to 0.47184. Adopt an explicit scene extent policy validated against measured registered geometry only once a depth/rejection and Macro gate is ready. [Bounds audit](03-environment-and-render-bounds.md)
3. **Share one material-compatible geometry bucket finalizer when a reference migration proves equivalence.** Architecture Rise is the first candidate. Keep layout, artistic materials, UV conventions and scene-specific choices with the callers. Defer common openings, roofs and vegetation until matching semantics and two actual use cases are evident. [Consolidation matrix](04-procedural-consolidation.md)
4. **Prefer scene-specific diagnostic inspection evidence.** An obstructed archival camera pose can be legitimate. Measure whether the teaching subject and practical public view remain accessible before considering runtime navigation changes. [Observer inspection](05-observer-inspection.md)
5. **Budget relative to the actual bottleneck and device sample.** Foreground has the most effective triangles but substantially fewer draw calls than Rise on this workload. Postprocessing can dominate the physical source draw. Track construction, source and post passes, resource residency and lifecycle separately. [Performance and ownership](06-performance-and-lifecycle.md)
6. **Defer lighting implementation.** Use controlled Table Tilt, Interior Corner, outdoor and Macro fixtures to separate aperture composition, representational light and assistance, material response and future bounce. The current opaque decorative glazing, room openings, outdoor clip and hard shadows affect which GI claims the test scenes can support. [Lighting and GI](07-lighting-gi-readiness.md)

The initial implementation should be **A1, Ground Glass diagnostic qualification**, with test fixtures and evidence only. The audited baseline also exposes two seeded-fixture comparator failures. These are separate from the observed Ground Glass ratio and must be handled explicitly when qualifying future migrations.
