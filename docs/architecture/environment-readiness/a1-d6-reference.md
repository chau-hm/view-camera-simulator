# A1 D6 candidate reference — declared before confirmatory captures

This bounded reference uses Architecture + Foreground, seed 284719 and seed 1, neutral standards and camera pitch, 150 mm lens, focus 9500 mm, f/11, Upright Assist, standard quality and ordinary WebGL. The fixed source/final readback is 655 × 524 RGBA8, bottom-left origin; Upright's observed transform is identity. The primary candidate assumed this rectangle was a single plaster surface; pixel-level first-hit inspection rejected that assumption because the window trim crosses it. The one bounded alternative is the same x=[317,330), y=[309,322) rectangle explicitly treated as a protected façade/window-trim edge, with those known surfaces as the reference feature. Confirmation must independently raycast every ROI pixel, accept only the front plaster and its window-stone/edge roles, and require the same first-hit identity and depth at every pixel across seeds. The relevant reference boundary is intentional; unrelated foreground occluders must be absent. Its projected location is well inside the image circle. The two material seeds may change raw colors and textures; each final region is compared to its own current raw source.

For parallel planes, v(D)=fD/(D−f) and signed diameter is (f/N)(v(F)/v(D)−1). With f=150 mm, N=11, F=9500 mm and the front-plaster D=9488 mm, the independent expected CoC is approximately −0.000277 mm, or 0.000714 pixel radius at 655/127 pixels/mm. The entire 48-pixel gather halo must be independently raycast per pixel and constrained to façade/glazing depths 9.4–9.7 m; propagating the conservative half-code 16-bit perspective-depth uncertainty through the same formula must bound its physical radius below 0.125 pixel. A captured full CoC readback is a separate execution witness, not the source of this expectation. The capture must establish the relevant halo's first-hit/depth and the ROI visibility; if it cannot, this candidate remains partial.

At this subpixel bound the far gather's zero-footprint path preserves the source, and the near gather's first noncentral proposal is at least 6 pixels away (48 px search radius, 32 samples) and cannot belong to a <0.125 px footprint. The processed sample should therefore preserve the same local detail, subject to the independently calculated composite gain. For each pixel, gain is `(150 / v(9500))² × (v(9500)² / (v(9500)² + x_mm² + y_mm²))²`; this accounts for f/11 reference throughput, bellows and the parallel cos-fourth illumination. The ROI is far inside the 110.7 mm image circle, so its coverage gain is one. A per-channel residual of at most 2 RGBA8 codes permits the three half-code UNORM conversion/accumulation errors and a small floating-point allowance; it is fixed from representation rather than measured candidate/fault separation. Source luminance standard deviation must exceed 4 codes, twice that per-channel error allowance, to give a useful local-detail witness. Compute processed and source local responses after gain compensation, not absolute RGB across seeds.

A comparator-only 11×11 box blur of the actual captured raw source is the declared local-detail-loss negative control: the same residual gate must reject it. This mutation is not an actual application rendering fault. The accepted depth-edge fixture's actual zero-blur and excessive-footprint GPU faults remain the separate execution-level controls. These assumptions, especially full halo depth/visibility and effect of linear filtering, must be checked before qualifying D6; a passing numerical comparison alone is insufficient.
