# Lighting and GI readiness

The accepted scenes provide useful controls for future lighting research. Table Tilt provides a real aperture, Interior Corner provides a represented point source, and the streets and courtyard provide contact surfaces and outdoor occluders. The immediate opportunity is a controlled lighting measurement fixture after the Ground Glass diagnostic and render-extent investigations. The current evidence supports retaining the existing lighting, exposure, and backend policies at baseline `35212892d5a359213f68f984bc68ad47c4520886`.

## Ground Glass brightness and local reflection

The WebGL physical path draws the registered subject, its illumination, and source color/depth from the physical camera. It classifies the circle of confusion (CoC), gathers near and far contributions, and composites them with relative aperture and bellows throughput, spatial optical illumination, and coverage. `(11 / N)²` is relative aperture throughput. Trusted extension adds `(f / v)²`. At equal extension, f/22 gives 0.25 and f/32 gives about 0.118 of the f/11 aperture throughput. These values are linear gains. Raw and Upright select display orientation, while the separate Raw RTT Debug bypass skips CoC and gather and uses neutral optical gains. Keep that distinction when comparing source radiance and final display. [Photographic gain](../../../src/core/optics/groundGlassIlluminance.ts#L15) · [RTT pass order](../../../src/render/GroundGlassRTT.tsx#L1660) · [Raw Debug gain](../../../src/render/GroundGlassRTT.tsx#L1720) · [Coverage bypass](../../../src/render/groundGlassCoverage.ts#L135) · [Optical illumination bypass](../../../src/render/groundGlassNaturalIllumination.ts#L25) · [Composite shader](../../../src/render/groundGlassDofShaderSources.ts#L404)

The historical PR #369 WebGL2 processed sidecars `baseline/gg-dof-f{22,32}-processed.json` and `cycle-2/validated/gg-dof-f{22,32}-processed.json` record 0.24192 at f/22 and 0.11435 at f/32 for both source trees at +2° and 6830 mm. The lower contrast and dark small-aperture captures are consistent with the existing gain. Those historical observations neither isolate GI nor measure absolute screen luminance. Record the renderer's actual output color settings and distinguish linear scene-target data from final images in any follow-up. Architecture Rise glazing currently uses opaque standard material with color `#233b43`, roughness 0.30 and metalness 0.12. Courtyard hero glazing, side-window panes and lantern panes are also opaque standard materials. A modeled recess or pane can shape visibility and shadows. It cannot establish light transmission into a room. The historical Rise Planar and Probe fixtures used earlier glazing and scoped geometry. They demonstrate a reflected-radiance and apparent-position seam, with bounded evidence for an ideal planar comparison. Retain the existing decision to defer local reflections. A future reflected contribution needs focus at its own apparent position through physical CoC and gather before linear radiance accumulation and a shared film composite. Mirror Shift is a separate compatibility control. Its RTT construction mounts a bounded virtual duplicate and reflected camera proxy while the Observer mounts the physical set. ## Backend boundary

The installed package is Three.js r187. Ordinary production Observer and Ground Glass paths use WebGL2. Explicit development Observer requests can attempt WebGPU for revision-qualified Architecture Rise and View Camera Anatomy, with lifecycle evidence required to identify native execution versus internal or application fallback. Architecture Rise's Observer qualification includes procedural environment and shadow-map support in the scoped verification record. The other audited lighting fixtures are unevaluated by that registry. Ground Glass uses a separate policy. Architecture Rise has a trusted development M3 Raw RTT pilot and scoped r187 M6 presentation evidence. M4/M5 research records do not independently authorize an attempt. Current M6 conformance does not qualify photographic quality, transplant to these indoor or Macro scenes, or authorize WebGPU in production. None establishes AO, diffuse GI or transmission readiness on either backend. ## Readiness decisions

- **Ambient occlusion can begin in a controlled contact fixture.** Compare known contact gaps under matched direct sources in both views, including a negative control which moves the occluder off-camera without moving its world position. Opening up a camera view can remove a screen-space input while leaving world-space geometry unchanged. Any dependence should be identified before qualifying a screen-space result. AO should preserve target detail and avoid repeating the same darkening already supplied by the directional shadow.
- **Indirect diffuse and color bleed need a reference case first.** Use an opaque diffuse donor and a neutral receiver with known sidedness, light orientation and occluder positions. Hold source, projection, optical gain and tone settings fixed. Measure source and receiver in linear light, with donor removed or neutralized and direct-only controls. An existing color texture, warm assist fill, hard shadow, emissive color or greater contrast at native size can explain an image change independently of a bounce.
- **Daylight and continuous lighting need explicit semantics.** Table Tilt's opening is suitable for verifying direct source visibility and varied aperture occlusion. Interior Corner better isolates an artificial source and enclosure error. A future experiment should specify source position and angular extent, length units, attenuation, visibility, and reference exposure. Treat enclosure and actual transmissive glazing as separate requirements. A portable lighting fixture can demonstrate source behavior without modifying either built scene.
- **Environment reflections and local reflections need different acceptance.** Keep the procedural Rise sky as a test of distant response. Reopen local reflection only for a scene with a meaningful need and a producer that can preserve independent apparent-position behavior in the physical consumer. Test clipping, the camera transition and off-camera occlusion under each renderer. Account for whether render targets and geometry are represented, visible, or owned by another consumer.
- **Advance only after the diagnostics and extent work.** Fix the capture-state identity and radiance interpretation first. Qualify indoor and outdoor geometry visibility and a Macro control before integrating indirect illumination. Preserve separate per-renderer resources with scoped teardown and add timing or memory budgets only once the measured pass has useful quality at app scale. Current limitations include partial shells, sparse exterior context, abrupt directional shadows, decorative glazing, and strongly attenuated Ground Glass at small apertures. Differences in material, sample scale and composition also need controls.
