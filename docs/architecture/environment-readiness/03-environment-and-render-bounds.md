# Teaching bounds and Ground Glass render extent

The physical Ground Glass camera has an existing render-bounds resolver. For ordinary scenes it falls back to canonical teaching bounds. The courtyard exposes a real limit of that fallback. Recovering the distant faces is geometrically possible with the same film rays. That change also replaces sky with depth samples in the image used by the DOF renderer. A future extent policy should require explicit scene opt-in and measured validation of the mounted geometry. Evidence in this document is pinned to `35212892d5a359213f68f984bc68ad47c4520886`.

## Current registered-geometry survey

The evidence package contains `bounds-survey.mjs`, its output log and `bounds-survey.json`. The script imports the current registered factories and canonical camera helper with Vite SSR, constructs and disposes each real registered RTT subject, and measures world vertices including instance transforms. Canonical reset uses the scene preset. Each unseeded state tests an 80 × 64 grid of physical film rays against the registered root. The grid separates a genuine no-hit cell from a hit excluded by the view-axis clip interval. Rasterization, material depth writes and shadows require pixel-level evidence. Interior Corner uses the full registered root because the room has no single dedicated environment group. The survey scope is the registered mesh root. Observer marks, render layers and texture transparency require separate evidence.

## Courtyard fixed-optics clip experiment

Using the same accepted courtyard geometry, the CPU survey changes only the renderer far plane from 13.53 m to 17.40 m. The near plane, canonical camera, focal length, film corners, apertures and world geometry remain common inputs. At the calibrated +20 mm/+2°/6830 mm/f11 state, the historical result of 395 clip-only cells is reproduced. Those rays first meet geometry between 13.805 and 15.622 m along the view axis. The far-hit mesh counts identify the east return and rear paving extension.

| State | Original clip-only → environment | Other subject hits preserved | Already-visible environment hits preserved | Open samples preserved |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Reset | 393 | 3,492 | 280 | 955 |
| Calibrated Tilt + Focus | 395 | 2,580 | 286 | 1,859 |
| High-tilt stress, +20 mm/+6.6°/7750 mm/f11 | 394 | 2,606 | 274 | 1,846 |

The per-cell transition matrix confirms that every other class is unchanged. The numerical change in the off-axis target NDC x/y mapping was zero for all tested states. The same nearer intersections still win, so the additional rays are far-clipping cases and do not erase existing first hits. True open rays remain clear. Geometry outside the lateral frustum cannot appear when only far changes. Occluded surfaces require an independent nearer-hit test. An asset missing from a surface-specific mount, such as Mirror Shift's deliberate Observer omission, requires an ownership audit.

### Browser replay on the current source

A Vite-local transform changed the physical far plane only. Browser replays used Architecture + Foreground seed 284719/schema 1, Chromium with ANGLE Metal on Apple M4 Pro, Standard quality, a 1440 × 1000 viewport at browser DPR 1 and a 655 × 524 physical target. The profile reported an effective RTT DPR of 1.5. Both far ranges rendered the full five-pass processed pipeline. Current, unmodified clipping reproduced the 395-cell calibrated result. The 17.4 m trace restored those 395 calibrated hits and preserved all closer first hits. At the high-tilt stress state it restored 394 hits, 361 on architectural geometry and 33 on added ground. Canonical sharpness and film x/y mapping matched. The processed/raw variance ratio went from 0.50962 to 0.47184 with both frames contentful. The ratio crossed the existing 0.50 limit while the camera controls and lesson optics stayed fixed. At calibrated Tilt + Focus, the ratio changed from 0.48755 to 0.45817. The existing 0.50 assertion applies to the stress state. A1 should qualify physical blur separately before A2 expands a production capture.

The 395 calibrated and 394 stress restorations also produced new raster depth at their sample pixels. On the common surfaces with valid packed depth in both runs, the absolute reconstructed depth shift had a median of 31.25 mm and p95 of 106.62 mm in both states (1,419 calibrated and 1,374 stress comparisons). The maxima were 195.76 mm and 181.20 mm respectively. Samples use the nearest 16-bit raster depth. Boundaries and quantization can contribute. An independent world-position reference is needed to attribute error to either configuration. Signed GPU CoC at matching building-base and building-middle probe pixels shifted by about +0.0024 mm in both states. Under stress those values were +0.01660 → +0.01907 mm and −0.30249 → −0.30005 mm respectively. Foreground probes in these frames had no in-frame sample. The canonical target sharpness scores still matched. This motivates a depth/reconstruction and CoC gate in addition to the existing film-mapping test.

Single source-pass CPU submission observations were 0.60 → 0.40 ms calibrated and 0.50 → 0.40 ms at stress. The GPU timing windows differ in frame count and capture history and give no controlled performance comparison. Review paired raw and processed captures, packed depth, timing windows, probe samples and renderer metadata in `captures/current-normal/`, `captures/current-far17p4/` and `reports/extent-comparison.json` in the evidence package.

### Depth-dependent risks

Changing near/far changes normalized depth and the inverse projection matrix. Matching projection and inverse should reconstruct the same world point with exact arithmetic for common visible surfaces. Raster quantization, clear-depth validity, world-position CoC and depth-based near/far gather remain separate risk surfaces. WebGL allocates a depth texture with unsigned-short preference and uses depth-code-dependent gather tolerance. WebGPU has an explicit backend clip-space conversion and separately observed depth attachment format. [Depth allocation](../../../src/render/groundGlassRttResources.ts#L80-L102) · [GLSL reconstruction](../../../src/render/groundGlassDofShaders.ts#L12-L26) · [GLSL depth rejection](../../../src/render/groundGlassDofShaders.ts#L493-L551) · [WebGPU camera](../../../src/render/webgpu/GroundGlassWebGPUAdapter.tsx#L1241-L1290) · [Native validity](../../../src/render/webgpu/groundGlassPhysicalCocNodes.ts#L239-L261)

For scale, the numerical survey projects a view-axis point, rounds depth to 16 or 24 bits and reconstructs with the inverse matrix. With a 10 mm near plane, the local 16-bit interval around 6.9 m is approximately 73 mm for both 13.53 m and 17.40 m far. Around the 0.4 m Macro depth it is approximately 0.24 mm for both 4 m and 17.4 m far. The 24-bit model gives approximately 0.284 mm and 0.00095 mm at those depths respectively. These ideal-model code steps describe quantization only. Use measured GPU depth and CoC to establish renderer tolerances. Raising near to 0.1 m substantially changes quantization as well as the minimum visible distance. This shows why clip policy and depth format must be examined together. The four Macro registrations use specimen depths of about 0.30–0.54 m and keep a 4 m far floor. Preserve those existing policies by default. At narrow depth of field, verify measured depth and signed CoC together near focal inversion, close occluders, silhouette boundaries, and a clear-depth sample. An outdoor far-only trial gives no basis to change the Macro near rule or depth format.

## Alternatives and recommendation

| Option | Benefit | Cost and failure mode |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Explicit declared bounds | Stable and inspectable. Existing `rttBounds` proves an opt-in seam can work without changing the scene definition or public focus range. | A declaration can drift after asset edits or seeded placement. It also needs a preservation test for frame fingerprints, render ownership and default clipping. |
| Automatic mounted bounds | Captures real dynamic geometry and can detect authored extent drift. | A generic union includes non-photographic helpers, artifacts of broad merged mesh AABBs, off-axis masses and intentional shadow/reflection-only pieces. It is only available after mount, can trigger invalidation or work on every frame, and may differ across Observer and RTT because their groups have different roles. |
| Explicit policy plus measured validation | Keeps a deterministic per-scene opt-in while giving declaration drift and coverage real tests. A bounded selector can use the intended registered geometry and variation identity. | Requires an explicit participation contract and a test for incoming request freshness. Each participating scene needs a reviewable physical extent and viewport behavior proof. |
| Existing policy and local placement | Already sufficient for sampled Rise, Oblique, Table Tilt and Interior Corner. It also preserves the intentional courtyard clearance from #369. | The accepted courtyard omits rear context from the physical capture, so scene layout has been constrained by an unrelated canonical AABB and an image-level stability assertion. |

**Recommend the hybrid option after A1 diagnostics qualification.** At the existing `resolveRenderBounds` seam, retain `scene.bounds` and all current overrides as defaults. Have optional scene presentation/asset metadata identify only the additional context intended to be photographed, with finite scene-unit bounds and an explicit cap. Validate that declaration against the opted-in registered geometry for relevant seeds, camera states and representation. Resolve it at the renderer boundary once per semantic asset/variation revision, then derive camera-relative near/far with the existing helper. Tie any resolved value or version that can affect output to the accepted frame identity. Keep broad automatic inference and changes to the default near plane out of the first trial. [Existing option seam](../../../src/render/groundGlassSceneProfiles.ts#L44-L65) · [Registered ownership](../../../src/render/sceneSubjectRegistry.tsx#L504-L523)

The names **teaching bounds**, **represented environment extent** and **physical render clip** describe responsibilities in this audit. Keep the existing `SceneDefinition.bounds` field as the teaching owner. Keep renderer-owned extent resolution at the existing named profile boundary. Use `rttBounds` for compatible per-registration declarations before adding interfaces. A future optional declaration should use millimetres, return an immutable value owned by the registration or presentation, and include deterministic identity with a separate renderer-side world-metre camera result. An environment can be absent. Ineligible or invalid metadata must fall back to the prior policy, with diagnostic evidence. Mounted geometry verification should respect visibility, draw role, layers, scene graph transforms and per-instance bounds. Cache measurements at construction or test time, keyed by actual asset and variant identity. Keep presentation assists, diagnostic helpers, out-of-shot geometry and renderer-owned resources outside normal photographic participation. Mirror Shift needs its existing explicit representation rule.

The first A2 proof should compare the current courtyard policy with one bounded opt-in at a fixed teaching state, then check reachable movement extrema, focus/aperture and both Ground Glass presentations. Require preservation of canonical optics and frame identity, correct known-surface/raster depth and CoC, comparable occluder rejection, and existing registration/disposal invariants. Keep Macro, Anatomy, Mirror and all unmodified scenes on the existing path. A1 needs to qualify image-level diagnostics first because a successful extent change can still alter the existing variance ratio. A2 can ship a dormant, legacy-equivalent seam or remain local until those gates pass. A scene can retain local placement without adopting an extent override. No shared rendering policy is implemented in A0.
