# Procedural construction and resource ownership

The four audited subjects already share millimetre conversion, asset registration, and instance disposal. A narrow geometry merge is the strongest new consolidation candidate. The scenes need different ways to produce compatible geometry, express openings, and preserve diagnostic identities. Their layout and finishes belong to their individual factories.

## Batching contracts

| Path | Bucket, coordinates and attributes | Bounds, ownership and migration risk |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rise `UrbanGeometry` | The caller supplies one group per building, sidewalk element or set of nearby props. Buckets use material object identity. The builder clears geometry groups, writes box rotations and offsets into vertices, applies the material's facade tile metadata where present, and retains ordinary primitive UVs in other cases. Roof triangles have their own normals and mapping. Indexed and non-indexed parts work in separate compatible buckets. | A singleton goes straight to a named mesh. Multiple parts merge in order and their geometries are disposed. The builder computes the resulting box and sphere and clears its buckets. A merge error currently occurs before the failing parts are disposed. Keep grouping, attribute preparation, mount order and the existing explicit bounds when extracting the merge. [Implementation](../../../src/render/architecture-rise/environment/UrbanGeometry.ts#L8) |
| Oblique `BuildingGeometry` | Emits indexed positions, normals, UVs and optional pane vertex colors directly from one box template. Builds in parent space with axis-aligned mm sizes and offsets. A string name selects a bucket that records the first material and parent. Its continuous UV rule uses position in metres along the face tangent. | Disposes the template and creates one geometry per named bucket. The supporting building code separately mounts roof panels and uses within-subject instances of barrel, ring and bollard shapes. Collapsing to material identity or translating into a common geometry merger would change semantics and allocation. Name collisions with different parents or materials are an implicit caller precondition. [Box emitter](../../../src/render/oblique-architecture/BuildingGeometry.ts#L16) · [Roof panels](../../../src/render/oblique-architecture/environment/IndustrialBuildings.ts#L110) · [Fixture reuse](../../../src/render/oblique-architecture/environment/IndustrialStreet.ts#L96) |
| Foreground `BuildingGeometry` | Creates a name-keyed bucket of prepared pieces, using the first material for that name. A single parent and deterministic name prefix are supplied by the wrapper. Boxes are clones of its local template with planar UVs based on local metres. Shape, foliage, roof and lathe emitters supply their own compatible attributes. | The per-elevation wrapper disposes its box template and merged inputs. It preserves singleton properties and leaves bounds to existing consumers. A swap of bucket identity, names, UV space or singleton geometry groups could alter both rendering and diagnostic lookup. [Named builder](../../../src/render/architecture-foreground/BuildingGeometry.ts#L13) |
| Foreground `batchBuilding` | A second pass traverses a fresh temporary tree. It transforms geometries with the temporary nodes' `matrixWorld` into local building coordinates and buckets by material object. The real building's translation and yaw are applied by the destination group. Wall extrusions explicitly receive sequential indices and clear groups. | Bounds are computed per building and material. The in-place matrix application assumes unshared temporary geometries. Transplanting this logic to a shared fixture or using final world coordinates would corrupt geometry ownership or apply a transform twice. Multi-material meshes are skipped in this narrow path. [Wall preparation](../../../src/render/architecture-foreground/environment/CourtyardBuildings.ts#L32) · [Final building batch](../../../src/render/architecture-foreground/environment/CourtyardBuildings.ts#L199) |
| Table Tilt selective merging | The sideboard merges pulls of one finish in cabinet-local coordinates. The tray combines a hollow extruded rim with a rounded floor. Most freestanding props and room elements retain individual meshes and names. | Temporary arrays are disposed after merge. Both sites assume their fixed input meshes are compatible and preserve different geometric roles. The pulls are a plausible later merge-helper consumer. The tray's attributes and continuous surfaces need their own equivalence evidence. [Sideboard pulls](../../../src/render/table-tilt/TableTiltStudioFurnishings.ts#L63) · [Recessed tray](../../../src/render/table-tilt/TableTiltFinishes.ts#L91) |

A mesh AABB represents a spatial envelope. It can include clear space between separate parts and the opening inside a wall. The current Oblique Architecture probe in `procedural/aperture-aabb.json` places a ray through a front aperture whose first glazed hit lies inside the structural mesh's AABB. The structure emits zero triangle hits on that ray. The legacy Architecture Rise probe-origin research fixture rejects origins by mesh AABB before scoring candidates. Broader batching could therefore affect such fixture decisions even with unchanged visible surfaces. Use AABBs for conservative culling and prefiltering. Use scoped solid geometry, triangle hits or explicit colliders to establish occlusion or clearance. [Probe-origin filter](../../../src/tests/e2e/fixtures/architecture-rise-probe-focus-reference-harness.ts#L1096) · [Oblique aperture test](../../../src/tests/unit/obliqueArchitectureMountedGeometry.test.ts#L188)

Historical courtyard measurements motivate local batching. In verified #369 archive `e36f90a639ad06a0c5e63e6dc497f99ada353b33aa7033f2766ee286b7a3f235`, `cycle-1/coverage.json` and `cycle-2/validated/coverage.json` report 328 → 116 environment meshes and 86,984 → 99,198 environment triangles. Reported reset draw calls go from 854 → 502 Observer and 493 → 254 Ground Glass. Both captures changed layout between cycles, including a physical clipping correction. Those counts establish that triangle count and draw calls moved in opposite directions. The simultaneous layout and clipping changes prevent isolating a causal batching speedup or deriving a general complexity limit.

## Seeds and owner boundaries

- Each registered Observer mount creates one group and pairs effect cleanup with the registered disposer. The R3F primitive sets `dispose={null}`. Ground Glass separately mounts a registered group and removes it before disposal. Both consumers receive matching semantic requests. A seed change replaces the owned instance. The renderer's subject lifecycle and state reporting remain distinct from intermediate geometry disposal. [Observer effect](../../../src/render/SceneAssetSubjects.tsx#L51) · [RTT subject mounting](../../../src/render/groundGlassSceneProfiles.ts#L69) · [RTT cleanup](../../../src/render/GroundGlassRTT.tsx#L1158)
- Rise's seed affects appearance through named role streams and affects the streetscape road tint through the ground color. Foreground and Table Tilt now also have schema-v1 Free Mode appearance descriptors at the audited revision. Foreground extensions use exactly the same seed-dependent paving material as the original plane. The courtyard's architecture and plant distribution use their own fixed data. Table Tilt's seed reaches tabletop, frame and teaching props, while the floor and studio room use separate fixed material construction. Oblique has no variation descriptor in its scene registration. [Rise resolver](../../../src/scenes/architectureRiseMaterialVariation.ts#L167) · [Foreground resolver](../../../src/scenes/architectureForegroundMaterialVariation.ts#L179) · [Table resolver](../../../src/scenes/tableTiltMaterialVariation.ts#L161) · [Scene registry](../../../src/render/sceneSubjectRegistry.tsx#L379)
- Material and texture reuse within one root is intentional. The disposer deduplicates geometries, materials and texture slots by object identity, including Rise's extra owned adaptation materials. Avoid passing live `BufferGeometry`, materials or textures between independent mounts. Design any future immutable CPU-data cache so each owner constructs its own GPU resources and receives private copies of any arrays that it may mutate. [Disposer](../../../src/render/TeachingMaterials.ts#L68) · [Rise special disposal](../../../src/render/ArchitectureRiseSubjectFactory.tsx#L940)
- The existing registry can dispose completed subjects. It lacks a returned root when a constructor throws before registration completes. Current merge sites also differ in when they release inputs on error. A new helper that accepts ownership needs one explicit consumption rule and a failure test. Previously emitted but unpublished results, transferred inputs and any borrowed materials require different treatment. Keep factory-owned resources attached to a root or explicitly tracked until ownership is transferred. [Registry creation](../../../src/render/assets/sceneAssetRegistry.ts#L402) · [Rise merge failure](../../../src/render/architecture-rise/environment/UrbanGeometry.ts#L85) · [Foreground merge failure](../../../src/render/architecture-foreground/BuildingGeometry.ts#L46) · [Courtyard merge failure](../../../src/render/architecture-foreground/environment/CourtyardBuildings.ts#L209)

## First construction PR

Start with a buffered merge utility scoped to a single static, single-material bucket. Adopt it only inside Architecture Rise's existing urban helper. The caller supplies ordered, owned and compatible geometry in one local coordinate basis. Keep the current position, normal, UV, index and color layouts, triangle ordering, geometry-group semantics, names, parent transforms, bounds and material identity. A singleton transfers directly to the caller. A merged result gets a new owned geometry and releases consumed input pieces. Specify incompatible-input handling and prove exactly-once release on both success and failure. Keep this utility private to geometry construction. Treat whole-scene collection, representation changes, automatic re-indexing, attribute synthesis, resource pooling and public APIs as separate proposals with their own evidence.

One reference-scene extraction is independently releasable after comparing current no-descriptor and seeded trees, preserving scoped mesh bounds and first-hit apertures, verifying material and UV signatures, and matching native Observer and physical Ground Glass views. Exercise a same-document scene exit and return and a seed change. A local batching operation can be judged by paired resource counts and draw calls at matched settings. A failure leaves the scene-local builder as the usable implementation and the next migration blocked. [Mounted Rise suite](../../../src/tests/unit/architectureRiseMountedGeometry.test.ts) · [Rise material variation](../../../src/tests/unit/architectureRiseMaterialVariation.test.tsx) · [Production seed replay](../../../src/tests/scene-variation-e2e/architecture-rise-seeded-materials-production.spec.ts)

Consider Foreground or the Table Tilt pulls after the first slice demonstrates equivalence. Keep Oblique's direct indexed emitter unless profiling and a concrete second compatible use establish a benefit. Follow with pure rectangular span subdivision or ground partition only after a caller-independent contract is verified with two actual cases.
