# PR 10C — Oblique Tabletop Compound Focus

- Branch: `feature/oblique-tabletop-compound-focus`; base: `origin/main` `bd6d113b127eb58f1acb89d6ab77124ea3ef5cb2`.
- Objective/scope: enable public Front Tilt + Front Swing + Focus for the Oblique Tabletop optical-solution slice; keep f/11 fixed, all unrelated movements unavailable, and defer the guided lesson and teaching overlays.
- Canonical source: `tabletopTopSurfacePlane`, `tabletopTransformBasis`, and the single `tabletopLocalToWorld` transform drive the mesh, markers, seven non-collinear surface samples, 3D subject, and RTT subject.
- Feasibility correction: the old PR10B plane had normal `(-0.021771, 0.987688, 0.154912)` and signed distance `112.85 mm`; with the shared 150 mm lens its required horizontal lens-normal magnitude was approximately `1.31`, so no real finite compound solution existed. The corrected transform uses `X=20°`, `Y=-35°`, and tabletop center `{x:0,y:-332,z:4550}` while preserving the `2800 × 3800 mm` tabletop and sample topology. Its normal is `(-0.196175, 0.939693, 0.280166)`, signed distance `1012.780 mm`, and required horizontal lens-normal magnitude `0.142176`.
- Continuous solution: derived analytically from `N·x=d` and the optical-axis-conjugate film model by aligning the lens normal's horizontal component with the subject-plane normal, then inverse-mapping through the shared `calculateLensNormal` convention. Result: Front Tilt `-8.000126°`, Front Swing `-1.681325°`, Focus `2447.497 mm`.
- Public solution: nearest shared steps (`0.1°`, `0.1°`, `10 mm`): Front Tilt `-8.0°`, Front Swing `-1.7°`, Focus `2450 mm`, f/11. No blind full-domain movement search is used as the calibration source.
- Physical evidence: final public CoC values for the seven samples (near-left, near-centre, near-right, middle, far-left, far-centre, far-right) are `[0.000913, 0.002685, 0.003714, 0.001146, 0.000764, 0.000363, 0.001223] mm`; maximum `0.003714 mm` is below the existing `0.1 mm` threshold. Recalibrated PR10B Tilt-only state is `-4.9° / 3310 mm` and remains incomplete with maximum `0.193679 mm`; the exhaustive public Tilt + Focus proof still finds zero fully aligned seven-sample states. At the compound focus, removing Swing raises the maximum to `0.242819 mm`, while the selected Swing solution reduces it to `0.003714 mm`; the opposite `+1.7°` Swing reaches `0.468242 mm` maximum.
