Its normal is `(-0.196175, 0.939693, 0.280166)`, signed distance `1012.780 mm`, and required horizontal lens-normal magnitude `0.142176`.
- Continuous solution: derived analytically from `N·x=d` and the optical-axis-conjugate film model by aligning the lens normal's horizontal component with the subject-plane normal, then inverse-mapping through the shared `calculateLensNormal` convention. Result: Front Tilt `-8.000126°`, Front Swing `-1.681325°`, Focus `2447.497 mm`.
- Public solution: nearest shared steps (`0.1°`, `0.1°`, `10 mm`): Front Tilt `-8.0°`, Front Swing `-1.7°`, Focus `2450 mm`, f/11. No blind full-domain movement search is used as the calibration source.
